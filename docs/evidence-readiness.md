# Evidence readiness

Evidence readiness connects each possible interpretation to the exact object that can support it.

## Directly supported

- the existence and size of three recurring national configurations of recorded activity;
- the feature patterns that distinguish those configurations;
- assignment stability under seeded repetition;
- profile movement under telephone and shorter-period specifications;
- practice-level association with selected public context measures;
- geographic composition across frozen reference-period ICBs.

## Supported with contextual qualification

Patient-experience, workforce, deprivation, rurality and population measures help explain how profile membership co-occurs with practice context. They remain ecological practice-level associations and use measure-specific available cohorts.

## Additional evidence required

Claims about resolved patient need, access pathways, workload reduction, safety, clinical quality, equity at patient level, supplier effects, economic value or causal impact require linked pathway, outcome, implementation or cost evidence.

The machine-readable [claim-to-evidence matrix](../outputs/tables/claim_to_evidence_matrix.csv) records the evidence source, cohort, disposition and language boundary for the writing authority.
